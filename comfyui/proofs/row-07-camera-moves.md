# Row 7. Camera moves, with amplitude and speed

**The claim.** The guide's closed camera vocabulary appears as English inside the shot — move,
amplitude, speed — never as a label stack.

**Verdict: green.**

## The graph

`row-07-camera-moves.api.json`. Two beats naming opposite corners of the vocabulary:

> a slow gentle push in on the lighthouse as the beam sweeps past, then a fast hard whip pan down
> to the waves breaking on the rocks below

## What came back

Both corners, all three dimensions, written into the action:

> the camera **pushes in with small amplitude at slow speed** toward the lighthouse tower as the
> powerful white beam … sweeps horizontally across the frame

> the camera **whip pans down with large amplitude at fast speed**, blurring the vertical lines of
> the tower into streaks before snapping into focus on the churning sea below

The vocabulary itself is schema-enforced at the planning layer (a non-member cannot be emitted) and
validator-enforced in the prose (P4/P5 family).

## Reproduce

Drag the api.json onto the canvas and press Run.
