# Row 18. A picture for clothing or a prop

**The claim.** A note beside a picture can hand the compiler a garment or a prop, and the document
tracks it — down to the sash.

**Verdict: green — and the document exceeded the old evidence: it merged the two plates into one
subject with per-picture reference lines.**

## The graph

`row-18-a-picture-for-clothing-or-a-prop.api.json`. Two subject slots with notes: the Geisha plate
("the deep red kimono with the floral sash, worn by the woman") and the dancer plate ("the pose,
one leg lifted and both arms raised above the head").

> a woman wearing the red kimono from @kimono holds the pose from @pose in an empty studio

> `<Picture 2>`. She wears a deep red kimono with a white collar … a wide gold and pink floral obi
> sash with a red cord, white tabi socks, and red zori sandals …

> hairstyle, and accessories.

The garment came through to the sash and the cord; the pose travels as a reference line rather
than a second person. (The same form is row 20's whole claim, measured there with a picture and a
