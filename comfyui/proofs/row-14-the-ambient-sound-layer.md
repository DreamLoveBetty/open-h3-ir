# Row 14. The ambient sound layer

**The claim.** Every non-silent brief carries a filled `overall_soundscape`: one paragraph of
ambience and physical sound, dialogue kept out of it.

**Verdict: green.** 15 of 15 documents compiled in this campaign so far carry a filled
`overall_soundscape` — including the silent-switch run, where the score goes and the ambience
stays (row 15). Dialogue appears in none of them; the layer holds rain, motors, footsteps,
crowd hum, surf, room tone.

This is a tally row: its evidence is every other row's document, in the `.api.json` files beside
these proofs, reproducible by running any of them.
