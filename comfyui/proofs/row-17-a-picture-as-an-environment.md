# Row 17. A picture as an environment

**The claim.** A picture marked "the setting" becomes ONE environment subject — the guide's own
construct — rather than being taken apart into props.

**Verdict: green — upgraded from the socket era, whose surface could not say "environment" at all.**

## The graph

`row-17-a-picture-as-an-environment.api.json`. One tray slot: the clean showroom as
`the setting`, note "the whole showroom, a location, not an object".

> the camera drifts slowly through @showroom as the city lights come up outside

## What came back

The construct, exactly:

> `<Subject 1>` is the spacious, empty interior showroom **environment** defined by `<Picture 1>`,
> featuring dark grey paneled walls on the left, floor-to-ceiling glass windows on the right, and a
> highly polished dark tile floor.

One environment, named as an environment. The socket era measured this same picture decomposing
into two props ("the circular platform", "the ceiling ring light") with no way to say otherwise;
the direct service call proved the role worked and the surface couldn't send it. The tray sends it.
Row 5's two-slot run confirms the same construct alongside a person subject, rendered.
